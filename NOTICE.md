The independent infrared observations are NOAA Climate Data Record (CDR) of Gridded Satellite Data from ISCCP B1 (GridSat-B1), Version 2, Knapp et al., NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. The paired inputs crop and sample the calibrated infrared-window brightness temperatures. [Dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.7289/V59P2ZKR)
